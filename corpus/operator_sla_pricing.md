# Amperia Charging Networks - Master Operator Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Commercial Pricing

**Document ID:** `SLA-OPERATOR-2026-T3`  
**Contract Version:** Version 3.4  
**Applicability:** Commercial Fleet Operators, Highway Concessionaires, and Retail Host Partners  

---

## 1. Network Uptime Commitments by Site Tier

Amperia guarantees monthly charging network availability (uptime) across all contracted public and semi-public charging plazas. Network uptime is calculated per site across all installed DC fast chargers (DC-150, DC-60) and AC destination units (AC-22).

$$\text{Monthly Uptime Percentage} = \frac{\text{Total Operational Port Hours} - \text{Unscheduled Downtime Port Hours}}{\text{Total Operational Port Hours}} \times 100$$

### 1.1 Site Classification Tiers
- **Tier 1 - Critical Highway Super-Hubs & Major Expressways:**
  - **Guaranteed Monthly Uptime:** **99.50%**
  - **Maximum Allowed Unscheduled Downtime:** 3.6 hours per month per charging port.
  - **On-Site Emergency Response SLA:** On-call field service technician must arrive on-site within **2 Hours** for critical multi-charger outages (≥ 2 down units or 50% site capacity loss).
- **Tier 2 - Commercial Retail Hubs, Malls & Transit Plazas:**
  - **Guaranteed Monthly Uptime:** **98.00%**
  - **Maximum Allowed Unscheduled Downtime:** 14.4 hours per month per charging port.
  - **On-Site Emergency Response SLA:** Field technician on-site response within **6 Hours**.
- **Tier 3 - Dedicated Fleet Depots & Destination Hospitality:**
  - **Guaranteed Monthly Uptime:** **95.00%**
  - **Maximum Allowed Unscheduled Downtime:** 36.0 hours per month per charging port.
  - **On-Site Response SLA:** Field service dispatch within **24 Hours** during standard working days.

## 2. Service Credit Schedule and Financial Penalties

If Amperia fails to meet the contracted monthly uptime commitment for a specific charging plaza, the site host/operator is entitled to contractual Service Credits applied against the subsequent month's operating fee invoice.

### 2.1 Tier 1 (Highway Sites) Service Credit Matrix
- **Monthly Uptime 97.00% to 99.49%:** **10% Service Credit** refunded on total monthly station management fee.
- **Monthly Uptime 95.00% to 96.99%:** **25% Service Credit** refunded on total monthly station management fee.
- **Monthly Uptime Below 95.00%:** **50% Service Credit** refunded on total monthly station management fee, plus waiver of network subscription dues.
- **Response Time Penalty:** If a Tier 1 multi-unit outage exceeds the 2-hour response window without an on-site technician check-in, an additional **5% penalty per hour of delay** is credited to the host.

### 2.2 Tier 2 & Tier 3 Service Credit Matrix
- **Tier 2 (95.00% to 97.99% Uptime):** **10% Service Credit**; Uptime below 95.00%: **20% Service Credit**.
- **Tier 3 (90.00% to 94.99% Uptime):** **5% Service Credit**; Uptime below 90.00%: **15% Service Credit**.

## 3. SLA Claim Process, Deadlines and Dispute Resolution

### 3.1 Claim Submission Deadline
All requests for service credits must be submitted formally in writing via the Amperia Operator Partner Portal (`portal.amperiacharge.com/sla-claims`).
- **Mandatory Claim Window:** Claims must be filed within **30 calendar days** following the end of the calendar month in which the downtime occurred.
- Claims submitted after the 30-day statutory window will be deemed waived and forfeit.

### 3.2 Permitted Force Majeure Exclusions
Unscheduled downtime caused directly by utility-side high voltage grid blackout, third-party civil vehicular collision with charger bollards, or scheduled maintenance approved with 48-hour advance notice is excluded from SLA downtime penalty calculations.
